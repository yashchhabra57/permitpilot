LAKEPORT MUNICIPAL CODE (SAMPLE), CHAPTER 14: RESIDENTIAL BUILDING PERMITS
This is a fictional sample ordinance written for demonstration. It is modeled on common small-city residential permit rules and is not the law of any real jurisdiction.

§ 14-101 Purpose and scope.
This chapter governs permits for decks, fences, accessory sheds, and roof replacement on one- and two-family dwellings within the City of Lakeport. Work on commercial buildings is governed by Chapter 15.

§ 14-102 Permit required.
No person shall construct, enlarge, or replace a deck, fence, accessory shed, or roof covering without first obtaining a permit from the Building Inspection Division. Ordinary repairs that replace less than 100 square feet of roofing are exempt.

§ 14-103 Application contents.
Every application shall include the property owner's signed authorization when the applicant is a contractor. Every application shall include a site plan drawn to scale showing property lines, existing structures, and the proposed work. A contractor shall provide proof of general liability insurance of not less than $500,000 at the time of application.

§ 14-104 Fees.
The permit fee for a deck is $150. The permit fee for a fence is $50. The permit fee for an accessory shed is $75. The permit fee for a roof replacement is $100. Fees shall be paid before the permit is issued and are not refundable after plan review begins.

§ 14-201 Decks.
A deck permit application must include framing plans showing joist size, joist spacing, beam size, and footing locations. Footings for decks shall extend at least 48 inches below finished grade. Decks more than 30 inches above grade shall have guards at least 36 inches high. A deck shall be set back at least 5 feet from any side lot line.

§ 14-202 Deck inspections.
A footing inspection is required before concrete is placed for any deck. A final inspection is required for every deck before it is used.

§ 14-301 Fences.
A fence permit application must include a survey or a site plan showing the fence location relative to property lines. Fences in a front yard shall not exceed 4 feet in height. Fences in a side or rear yard shall not exceed 6 feet in height. The finished side of a fence shall face the neighboring property or street.

§ 14-401 Accessory sheds.
An accessory shed permit application must include a site plan showing the shed footprint and its distance from the principal dwelling. A shed shall be set back at least 3 feet from rear and side lot lines. Sheds larger than 200 square feet shall be placed on a frost-protected foundation. A final inspection is required for every accessory shed.

§ 14-501 Roof replacement.
A roof replacement permit application must state the roofing material and the number of existing layers. No more than two layers of roof covering are permitted; when two layers exist, all layers shall be removed before new covering is installed. Ice barrier underlayment shall extend at least 24 inches inside the exterior wall line. A final inspection is required for every roof replacement.

§ 14-601 Plan review and corrections.
The Building Inspection Division shall complete plan review within 10 business days after a complete application is received. When corrections are requested, the applicant shall submit revised documents within 30 days or the application is considered abandoned.

§ 14-602 Permit expiration.
A permit expires if work has not started within 180 days after issuance. A permit also expires if the final inspection has not been requested within 365 days after issuance. An expired permit may be renewed once for a fee of $25.

§ 14-603 Inspection scheduling.
Inspections shall be requested at least one business day in advance. The permit card shall be posted in a location visible from the street until the final inspection is approved.
